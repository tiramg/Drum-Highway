How to test this prototype

Connect your drum module to the computer by USB MIDI, then choose Connect MIDI. In the kit panel, click Learn and strike the corresponding pad. Use the arrows to move lanes. The yellow hi-hat and red snare pads remain recognizable in any layout. Blue bars across the highway cue the kick.

Hit notes as they reach the bright line. Perfect = within 50 ms; Good = within 120 ms. Timing offset adjusts where incoming hits are judged: positive values compensate for late input. Pause and adjust if hits consistently read early or late. The groove is synthesized and changes tempo with playback speed.

This version plays a built-in groove and community drum charts for New Orleans Is Sinking and Billie Jean synchronized to YouTube playback. Automatic drum transcription is not implemented. MIDI availability depends on browser, permissions, and module support. If access fails, open this page directly in a MIDI-capable browser.
